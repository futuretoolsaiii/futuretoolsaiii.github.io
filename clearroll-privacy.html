ClearRoll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-28

Summary

ClearRoll analyzes your photo library and, if you choose, your contacts entirely on your device. Your photos, videos, thumbnails, contacts and the results of the analysis are never uploaded, sold or shared. ClearRoll has no servers of its own and no user accounts.

Photos and videos

  • ClearRoll asks for access to your photo library to find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, large videos and Live Photos.
  • All analysis (image fingerprints, sharpness, face quality) runs on your iPhone or iPad. Results are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app or use "Reset analysis cache".
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. Deletion uses Apple's Photos framework, so iOS asks you to confirm and items go to Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • If you turn on "Download iCloud thumbnails", or view a photo full screen, iOS may download a small copy of a photo stored in your own iCloud Photos. That transfer is between your device and Apple; ClearRoll receives nothing.
  • Compressed videos and converted Live Photos are saved back to your own library.

Contacts

  • Contacts access is optional and only requested when you open Duplicate Contacts.
  • Duplicates are found on your device by comparing phone numbers, emails and names. Contact notes are never read.
  • Before any merge, a backup file of your contacts is saved in the app's own storage on your device. You can share it yourself; ClearRoll never sends it anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ClearRoll is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect a device identifier, advertising data and approximate location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, Google may use the advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EEA and UK, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy choices. Ads never receive your photos, videos or contacts. See Google's advertising policy.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

ClearRoll sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics: standard app events (first open, sessions, app version, device model, iOS version, country) and a few feature events with counts only, such as "cleanup completed, 12 items". These never include photos, file names, library identifiers, contact data or anything about what is in your library. The advertising identifier is not collected by Analytics. See Firebase privacy.

Children

ClearRoll is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights and contact

Because ClearRoll keeps your photo and contact data on your device, deleting the app removes it. For questions or requests about data processed by Google, you can also contact us and we will help.
