Mission Alarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-29

Summary

Mission Alarm is an alarm clock that keeps ringing until you finish a short mini-game. Your alarms, wake-up history, streak and settings are stored only on your device. Mission Alarm has no servers of its own and no user accounts.

Alarms and wake-up history

  • Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit. iOS asks for your permission the first time; you can change it in Settings.
  • Alarm times, labels, chosen sounds and missions, and your wake-up log (wake time, mission duration, snoozes) are saved in the app's storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Puzzles are generated on your device. Nothing you type or tap in a mission leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Mission Alarm is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, only after you have woken up: never while an alarm is ringing or during a mission. Google may collect a device identifier, advertising data and approximate location (from your IP address) to show and measure ads. If you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, Google may use the advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EEA and UK, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice in Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. Ads never receive your alarms or wake-up history. See Google's advertising policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

Mission Alarm sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase Analytics: standard app events (first open, sessions, app version, device model, iOS version, country) and a few feature events, such as "mission completed: Quick Math, easy, 28 seconds". Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports (stack trace, device model, iOS version) so we can fix bugs. These never include alarm labels, your name or contact details. See Firebase privacy.

Children

Mission Alarm is meant for a general audience. Its Kids mode is a simpler alarm setting a parent can choose; it does not change what data is collected. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your rights and contact

Deleting the app removes all alarm and wake-up data from your device. For questions or requests about data processed by Google, contact us and we will help.
