VoiceNote AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-03

Summary

VoiceNote AI records, transcribes and summarizes audio entirely on your device. Your recordings, imported files, transcripts, titles, summaries and action items never leave your iPhone and are never sent to us or to anyone else. No account is needed.

Recordings and transcripts

  • The microphone is used only while you record. Recordings are stored in the app on your device.
  • Transcription runs on your iPhone with an on-device speech model. The model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face; that download contains no information about you or your recordings.
  • Summaries are created on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence on supported devices.
  • Audio or video you import from Files or Photos, or share to the app, is copied into the app and processed the same way. Photo library access is limited to the video you pick.
  • You can export or delete any recording at any time. Deleting the app deletes all of its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never have access to your recordings or transcripts.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and where required by law, we ask for your consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Google may collect device information, approximate location from your IP address and ad interaction data to show and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • With your consent, we collect anonymous usage counts such as "recording saved" (with a duration range), "transcription finished" (success or not), "summary created" and the export format. These events never contain audio, text, titles or file names.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) include technical details about the crash and your device model and OS version.
  • Both can be turned off in the app's Settings. In regions that require consent, analytics stay off unless you agree.

Children

VoiceNote AI is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and contact

Because your recordings and transcripts stay on your device, we cannot see, access or delete them for you; you control them in the app. For questions about this policy or your data, contact us.
